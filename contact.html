FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is NovaQuiz free to use?
Yes — all core practice exams are completely free. We may introduce optional premium question packs later.
Do I need an account to take an exam?
You can preview and take exams without an account, but signing up lets you save your results and track progress over time.
Can I retake an exam?
Absolutely. Questions are reshuffled on every attempt, so retaking an exam still feels fresh.
How is my data stored?
In this demo, your account and exam history are stored locally in your browser using localStorage.
